Kaay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Overview

Kaay is a personal productivity app that runs entirely on your device. Your privacy is straightforward: we do not collect, transmit, or share any of your data.

Data Storage

All data you create in Kaay — including tasks, reminders, tags, and photos — is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. Nothing is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Data Collection

Kaay does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-up is required
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No network requests of any kind
  • No crash reporting to external services

Permissions

Kaay requests the following Android permissions, all used locally:

  • Exact alarms — to schedule task reminders at precise times
  • Notifications — to display reminder notifications on your device
  • Boot completed — to reschedule reminders after a device restart
  • Camera (optional) — to take photos for task cards, stored on-device only

Third-Party Services

Kaay does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. There is no data sharing with any third party.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored on your device, you have full control. You can delete individual items within the app, or uninstall the app to remove all associated data.

Children's Privacy

Kaay is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
